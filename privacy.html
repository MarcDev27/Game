BACKUP 7105 · PRIVACY

Applies to the Backup 7105 app for iPhone and the web version of the game.

No data is collected

Backup 7105 does not collect, transmit or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to any server while you play.

Your saves stay on your device

Characters, worlds and settings are stored only on your device. The iPhone app also keeps a safety copy of your saves in its own storage, which is included in your device's iCloud or computer backups like any other app data.

Backups you make

When you choose Back up all saves, the game creates a backup file and opens the share sheet. The file goes only where you send it, for example iCloud Drive via Save to Files. Restoring reads a file you pick.

Contact
